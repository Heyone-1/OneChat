Guidelines & FAQ

Community Guidelines

OneChat is built for authentic, respectful conversations. By using OneChat, you agree to:

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, or bullying.
  • Stay authentic. Don't impersonate others or use bots.
  • No spam. Don't post repetitive or misleading content.
  • Keep it clean. No NSFW, violence, or illegal content.
  • Respect privacy. Don't share others' personal information.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn XP?
You earn XP for posting (+5), commenting (+3), liking (+1), following someone (+2), and getting followed (+5).
What is a "Top Fan"?
A Top Fan is the user who has commented the most times on a specific post. They're highlighted in purple below the post.
How do I message someone?
Visit their profile and tap the "Message" button. You can also tap the message icon in the top bar to see all your conversations.
What does the green dot mean?
A green dot means the user is currently online (active in the last 2 minutes). A grey dot means they are offline.
How do I switch themes?
Go to Settings → Preferences → Theme. Toggle between Light and Night mode anytime.
Can I delete my account?
Yes. Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This will permanently erase your profile, posts, comments, and likes.
How do I pin a post?
Tap the three dots (⋮) on your own post and select "Pin post". You can pin up to 4 posts to the top of your profile.
